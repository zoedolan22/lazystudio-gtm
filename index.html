What it takes to earn R20,000 each

You need R45,000 in monthly revenue. That pays R20,000 to each of you and leaves R5,000 as a working allowance for tax and software. An accountant can confirm the real figure.

Percentage fees on R5,000 to R12,000 of ad spend are too small. A 20% fee on R8,000 earns R1,600 a client, which takes about 28 clients to reach R45,000. A flat monthly retainer that includes design, copy and reporting gets you there with far fewer.

Hours assume 60 hours a week, about 260 a month. Selling, admin and the studio's own content use some of them, so plan on fewer. The R10,000 row rounds 4.5 clients up to 5.

Start near R5,000. At R8,000 of ad spend, a R7,500 retainer costs the client almost as much as the ads, which is a hard sale to a new brand. Keep each account near 30 hours, then raise revenue per client with add-ons and bigger budgets once results show. Set the price in week 1.

Who you sell to

B2C product brands, small to medium, Cape Town first, spending R5,000 to R12,000 a month on ads. More is better.

Your product wishlist is supplements, skincare, homeware, coffee and locally made products. Pick the first 40 prospects from those categories.

Where the 40 come from

  • Meta Ad Library. Search South Africa, active ads, one category at a time. A brand already running ads has a budget and gives you something to review.
  • Liam's events world. Bars, venues, promoters, coffee shops, and the drinks and clothing brands that sponsor events.
  • Zoë's network. Founders and marketers you know outside your current agency's clients.

Liam builds 25 of the 40 and Zoë builds 15. The list is due Wednesday 11 November.

Liam's property marketing background also fits estate agents and developers, who run lead ads on Meta. Take one property client in the first 90 days if the chance comes, because Liam has two years of agency work to point to there.
